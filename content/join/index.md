    ## **The global home for DoK practitioners**

    The Data on Kubernetes Community (DoKC) exists to advance the use of Kubernetes for stateful and data workloads, and unite the ecosystem around data on Kubernetes (DoK).

    It is a vendor-neutral, independent community of end users seeking best practices and solutions for running stateful and data applications on Kubernetes. We deliver a range of community programs designed to grow and support the burgeoning space.
