Kubernetes has the potential to change the game by enabling users to run complex databases anywhere they want. Its portability coupled with a SaaS user experience makes it a powerful tool.

In this DoKC session, [Robert Hodges](https://www.linkedin.com/in/berkeleybob2105), the CEO of [Altinity](https://www.linkedin.com/company/altinity?), talks about how his team built a portable [ClickHouse cloud service](https://clickhouse.com/cloud/) on Kubernetes and how it could create a new breed of managed databases.
