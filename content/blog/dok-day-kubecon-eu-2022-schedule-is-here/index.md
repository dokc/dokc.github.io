The schedule for [Data on Kubernetes Day](/dok-day-europe-2022-kubecon/) at KubeCon EU 2022 is finally here! Make sure to [register](https://cvent.me/5aYQ2E?rt=aJnC4AParEWJg_xsQ0A9Tg) to learn from experts at the forefront of running data on Kubernetes. This online event will feature 31 talks, 3 panels, and tons of entertainment in-between sessions.

The schedule can be viewed on the event page **[here](/dok-day-europe-2022-kubecon/)**.

Shout out to [**anynines**](https://www.anynines.com/), [**DataStax**](https://www.datastax.com/), [**EDB**](https://www.enterprisedb.com/), [**Ondat**](https://www.ondat.io/), and [**Timescale**](https://www.timescale.com/) for sponsoring the event!
