​​During this day, we learned from [Anynines](https://www.anynines.com/)’s CEO Julian Fischer about [Kubernetes operator lifecycle management](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_lQhoCUQReU). The ability to extend Kubernetes with Custom Resource Definitions and respective controllers has led to the OperatorSDK, which became the de facto standard for data service automation on Kubernetes.[DataStax](https://www.datastax.com/)’s Principal Solutions Architect’s Matt Overstreet shared how he managed [to scale Cassandra on EKS to over 1000 nodes and 20 million transactions per second](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P6vTnhXFaDQ). Not an easy achievement, which came with a lot of learnings.
[EDB](https://www.enterprisedb.com/) CTO of Cloud Native’s Gabriele Bartolini explained why companies [should run their Postgres clusters on Kubernetes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XRD5-V6907A). The company just open-source their PosgreSQL operator, [CloudNativePG](https://cloudnative-pg.io/), and submitted the project to the CNCF.

[Ondat](https://www.ondat.io/) Principal Developer Advocate’s Nic Vermande demonstrates how to use [Kustomize and Tekton to provide Kube-Native automated workflows](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZtyOViJeFdI) taking into account new parameters such as database operators, StorageClass and PVC.

[Timescale](https://www.timescale.com/) Cloud Engineer Oleksii Kliukin shared what they [learned from running a PostgreSQL managed service on Kubernetes on AWS](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1Es-S85xRE) for over a year, the benefits and drawbacks.

Last but not least, we [announced](/blog/announcing-the-dok-landscape/) the [Data on Kubernetes Landscape](/landscape/).
