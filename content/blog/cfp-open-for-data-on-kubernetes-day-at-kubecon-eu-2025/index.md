We're excited to announce that the Data on Kubernetes (DoK) Community will once again host a co-located event at KubeCon EU 2025 in London. As the leading gathering for DoK practitioners and enthusiasts, DoK Day showcases cutting-edge innovations and fosters invaluable knowledge sharing within our vibrant community.

## **Call for Presentations Now Open**

Are you pushing the boundaries of what's possible with data on Kubernetes? We want to hear your story! We're seeking compelling presentations in these key areas:

- Production DoK Use Cases and Best Practices
- Day 2 Operations and Maintenance Insights
- Emerging Technologies and Innovations
- Enabling Data Applications for:
  - Machine Learning
  - Artificial Intelligence
  - Streaming Workloads
  - Analytics Platforms

## **Important Dates to Remember**

📅 **Key Milestones:**

- October 22, 2024: CFP Opens, Sponsorship Opportunities Available, Registration Begins
- December 4, 2024: CFP Closes
- January 21, 2025: Speaker Notifications
- January 23, 2025: Schedule Announcement
- April 1, 2025: DoK Day Event

## **Why Attend DoK Day?**

DoK Day offers a unique opportunity to:

- Connect with fellow data enthusiasts and Kubernetes practitioners
- Learn from real-world implementation stories
- Discover the latest tools and technologies in the DoK ecosystem
- Share your experiences and contribute to the community's growth

## **Ready to Join Us?**

Visit the [Data on Kubernetes Day EU 2025 webpage](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/kubecon-cloudnativecon-europe/co-located-events/data-on-kubernetes-day/) for complete details about:

- Session submissions
- Registration information
- Sponsorship opportunities
- Event schedule (coming January 23)

Don't miss this chance to be part of the premier event for the DoK community. Whether you're a seasoned practitioner or just starting your DoK journey, this event promises valuable insights and meaningful connections.

We can't wait to welcome you to London for another exceptional DoK Day. Join us in shaping the future of data workloads on Kubernetes!
