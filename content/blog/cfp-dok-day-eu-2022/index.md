We are back at it again! As Kubecon Europe 2022 is quickly approaching, [we are looking for speakers](https://sessionize.com/data-on-kubernetes-day-eu-2022) who can share their knowledge about the art of running stateful workloads on Kubernetes. Happening on Monday, May 16, from 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM CET, the event will be 100% virtual and will feature speakers from all over the world.

We’re looking for vendor-neutral talks from practitioners that describe the use cases, techniques, and best practices for running data on Kubernetes. In addition, we will be looking for submissions from underrepresented and marginalized groups and will not accept submissions with all-male panels.

Not sure what topic to pitch? Get inspired by watching some of the [previous DoK Talks](/blog/category/dok-day-kubecon-na-2021/), and feel free to brainstorm with us by joining [our Slack](https://go.dok.community/slack)!

We invite you to submit talk proposals about:

- Solutions to technical challenges, patterns, and practices
- Validated use cases for running data on Kubernetes
- Learnings and experiences of running data on Kubernetes
- Trends that could help others validate their DoK software roadmap

The CFP is closing in a month, on March 16th, [so get your proposal](https://sessionize.com/data-on-kubernetes-day-eu-2022) in quick!
