DoKC is coming to London! Our community is international, and we have been operating fully online. As the community is growing and running stateful workload on Kubernetes is on its way to becoming an industry-standards, we are increasing our impact by launching local chapters with in-person events, starting with London!

The Data on Kubernetes Community (DoKC) was founded to lead discussions and share best practices about running stateful workloads on Kubernetes. The DoK London chapter, created by DoK sponsor [Ondat](https://www.ondat.io/), will welcome participants through live streams and IRL events.

The chapter is kicking off their [first meetup on Feb 16th](https://meetup.com/dok-london/events/283676237/%E2%80%A6) with a lab from Ondat’s DevRel [Nicolas Vermandé](https://twitter.com/nvermande) & and HashiCorp’s co-founder, Communication and Events lead [Dominique Top](https://twitter.com/devopsdomi). Their workshop will demonstrate how to unravel the key to your Kubernetes secret. They will be joined by TimescaleDB’s Community Manager [Lorraine P](https://twitter.com/atomicmutton), and Kasten’s Senior Global Developer Relations [Alison Dowdney](https://twitter.com/alisondowdney) for a panel discussing what difference communities can make in communities such as the Data on Kubernetes one.

Make sure to sign up [here](https://meetup.com/dok-london/events/283676237/%E2%80%A6). Let’s go [#DoKLondon](https://twitter.com/hashtag/DoKLondon?src=hashtag_click)! 🚀
