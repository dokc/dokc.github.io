CamData 3D, one of the newest projects of [Kurago](https://kurago.software/en/) in partnership with [Bystronic](https://bystronic.com/che/fr), provides a multi-tenant cloud service aiming to improve the digitization of the sheet metal industry. In this talk, [Senior DevOps Engineer Elie Assi](https://www.linkedin.com/in/elieassi/?originalSubdomain=es) elaborates on why and how they choose to host their service in Kubernetes instead of virtual machines.
