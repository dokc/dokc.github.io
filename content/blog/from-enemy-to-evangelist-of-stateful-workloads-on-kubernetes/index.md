Kubernetes was originally designed to run stateless workloads. Today, it is increasingly used to run databases and other stateful workloads.

[Rick Vasquez](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rickvasquezii/) used to be a huge opponent of putting any data in Kubernetes, now he is one of the most vocal proponents of doing so. Learn why he changed his mind.
