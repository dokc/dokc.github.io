In this talk, [OpsVerse](https://opsverse.io) co-founder [Arul Jegadish](https://www.linkedin.com/in/arul-jegadish-francis/) explains why they decided to switch from using [Jaeger](https://www.jaegertracing.io) storage backend to [ClickHouse](https://clickhouse.com), and why they choose to run it on [Kubernetes](https://kubernetes.io). He also shares tips and tricks to migrate and run it without problems.
