There is confusion amongst Kubernetes users as to when one should make a Deployment with a PVC and when they should use a StatefulSet with a PVC. There is also a general lack of understanding when it comes to disk access policies, what RWO/RWX means, and what they allow you to do. These concepts are complicated and require a deep level of understanding in order to avoid users making bad decisions that they come to regret later.

In the talk, Neil Cresswell and Bart Farrel explore when one should use each type and what things they need to think about before making a decision. They also explore in detail how to safely deploy a multi-replica application with persistence using Portainer.

As part of the talk, they explore disk access policy, understand what RWO really is and how RWX disk access changes the equation in regards to persistence.
