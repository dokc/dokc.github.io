After five years of community research, the data tells a clear story: **Data on Kubernetes (DoK) has won.** Running data workloads on Kubernetes is now standard practice across industries — the next chapter is about achieving operational excellence.

Based on a global survey of 182 technology professionals, the 2025 report shows that organizations have moved beyond adoption to focus on **cost optimization, performance, and AI/ML infrastructure at scale.**

### **Key Findings**

#### **DoK Reaches Full Production Maturity**

Nearly half of organizations now run 50% or more of their data workloads on Kubernetes in production, with leaders surpassing 75%. Over 60% attribute more than 10% of their company’s revenue to these deployments — clear evidence that DoK has become mission-critical infrastructure.

#### **AI/ML Workloads Are Driving the Next Wave**

While databases remain the top workload for the fourth consecutive year, AI/ML workloads have surged to 44% adoption. A remarkable 77% of organizations now view vector databases as critical infrastructure, signaling the rise of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures and real-time data systems.

#### **Cost Optimization Tops 2025 Priorities**

For the first time, cost optimization surpasses all other priorities, with 50% of AI/ML-focused organizations citing storage costs as their biggest challenge. Teams are implementing strategies such as GPU utilization optimization, tiered storage, and cross-zone data transfer reduction to control expenses as workloads scale.

#### **The Edge + Real-time Shift**

Organizations are re-architecting for real-time and distributed systems: 61% say edge computing is essential to their data strategy. 64% cite real-time data processing as critical to AI success. This marks a decisive move away from centralized, batch-oriented data systems.

#### **Skills and Performance Gaps Remain**

Despite maturity, 40% of organizations report a skills gap, and storage I/O performance remains the top bottleneck. Closing these gaps — through better tooling, training, and ecosystem collaboration — will define the next phase of DoK excellence.
