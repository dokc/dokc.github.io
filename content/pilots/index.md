    - **30 Pilots in 2027:** 15 start in March, 15 in September

    ## What Pilots do

    - Represent DoK at conferences, meetups and online
    - Pair with new contributors and reply within 48 hours
    - Ship at least one contribution a quarter: a pattern, a review, a talk or a Dock event
    - Help run the [Contributor Program](/contribute/) week to week
